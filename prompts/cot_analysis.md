After further tests in using the chain of thought prompting technique, I can say that it is powerful technique that helps the AI/LLM model to think more carefully resulting to a better and more concise response e.g:

- basic question: To find one-third of 20, you simply multiply 20 by 1/3. In mathematics, when you want to write a fraction as a division, you can flip it and put a denominator in front instead of below:\n\n1/3 _ 20 = (1 _ 20) / 3 = 6\\\_\\frac{2}{3}\n\nSince the result is not a whole number, it's usually expressed as a mixed number. In this case, 6 and 2/3 are the whole number part and fractional part respectively.

- chain of thought prompting: The third of twenty (20) is 6 and 2/3 (or 6.67 in decimal form). This can be calculated by dividing 20 by 3, which results in approximately 6 with a remainder of 2/3.

As we can see, the answer of the AI is more concise resulting to a lesser token count output.
